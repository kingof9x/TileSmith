How TileSmith works

A backstage tour of the code in 23 parts. Each part explains what that piece of the app does, then walks through the real code that does it: startup, settings, the render loop, test patterns, data and power wiring, the crew animation, NDI® output, video recording, exports and releases. Written for power users and sysadmins who are curious about code. Some parts are interactive.

Pixel maps

About 25 test patterns, tile IDs, pixel addresses and position arrows, sent out live over NDI®, Spout or Syphon.

Engineering

Weight, power, processor ports, data and power wiring for dozens of real tiles and processors, with printable cable labels.

Show files

Export PNG, layered PSD and SVG, PDF spec sheets, video and Resolume presets. Everything saves by itself.
